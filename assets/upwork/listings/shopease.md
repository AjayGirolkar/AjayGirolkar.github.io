## Description

I build native iPhone shopping apps in SwiftUI — catalog, search, product detail, cart, checkout.

A store app lives or dies on two things: how fast the grid scrolls, and whether checkout ever drops a customer. Eleven years of iOS, most of it on payments and banking inside global financial firms, means I build image caching and the failure states in from the start rather than patching them after your first bad review.

**What you get**

- Production SwiftUI screens, not prototypes
- Catalog, search, filters and product detail
- Cart and checkout flow, Apple Pay ready
- Shopify, WooCommerce or your own REST API wired up
- Image caching so the grid scrolls at 120fps on ProMotion
- Empty, loading and error states designed, not skipped
- Light and dark mode, Dynamic Type, VoiceOver labels
- A Loom walkthrough of the code and how to extend it

**How it runs**

Day 0 you send designs, your commerce backend and API keys. Day 2 the design tokens, navigation and product model land and you get a build to run. From there catalog, detail, cart and checkout go up against your live data, pushed daily to your repo. At handoff you get a code walkthrough, your revision round, and a checklist for App Store submission.

**About the screenshots**

The screens shown are from ShopEase, a reference app I designed and built myself to demonstrate this exact stack. It is not client work — it is the standard your build is held to.

## FAQ

**Which commerce backends do you support?**
Shopify Storefront API, WooCommerce REST, and any custom REST or GraphQL API. Tell me which at kickoff.

**Do you handle payments?**
I wire up Apple Pay and your existing payment provider's SDK. I do not build a payment gateway or handle PCI certification — that stays with your provider.

**Will the app work with my current website?**
Yes, as long as your store exposes an API. The app reads the same catalog, so your products stay in one place.

**Can you handle a large catalog?**
Yes. Pagination, image caching and prefetch are standard, not extras. Thousands of SKUs scroll fine.

**Will I own the code?**
Yes. Full source, in your repo. No license fees, no runtime tied to me.

**What if I don't have designs?**
Send a reference app or a rough sketch. I design to a token system either way — that is included.

**Do you build Android too?**
No. Native iOS only. That focus is why the iOS build is good.

**Can we talk before I order?**
Yes. Message me with your store platform and screen list and I will tell you which tier fits, or say honestly if a custom job is the better route.

## Project steps (workroom checklist)

Steps 1 and 2 are Upwork's own bookends. These are the ones you add in between. Tick each in the
workroom as it lands — same list for all three tiers.

1. **Requirements locked**
   You send designs, your store platform, API docs and a test key. The delivery clock starts once
   this is complete.
2. **Foundation build**
   Design tokens, navigation and the product model land. You get an Xcode project that builds
   clean and a build you can run on device.
3. **Storefront built**
   Catalog, product detail, cart and checkout built against your live data, including empty,
   loading and error states. Pushed to your repo daily.
4. **Polish pass**
   Light and dark mode, Dynamic Type, VoiceOver labels, and Apple Pay if it is in scope.
5. **Walkthrough delivered**
   Loom video of the code, the data layer, and how to add new screens.
6. **Revision round**
   You review and send one consolidated list of changes. I fix, you confirm, we close.
